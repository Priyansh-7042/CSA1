# Minecraft Coding (Rising Techies)

Welcome to **Minecraft Coding (Rising Techies)**! This course is designed to guide students aged 11-14 on their journey to master technology and software development. Over a duration of 3-9 months, students will complete 72+ immersive lessons packed with hands-on projects, interactive quizzes, and logical problem solving.

## What is this course about?
Code in the Minecraft world using fun, interactive block-based and Python tools. With our expert-guided curriculum, students learn real-world applications of logic, problem-solving, and creative styling. By compiling multiple projects throughout the class, kids transition from being tech consumers to tech creators.
